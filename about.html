About

Who are we?

Silamana Business Model AGENCY (SBM) is a Guinean digital transformation and AI strategy agency specialized in supporting African SMEs, entrepreneurs and project founders.

Founded by Mamoudou Sangaré, a project structuring and AI strategy consultant, Silamana supports entrepreneurs, SMEs and creators in their digital transformation and competitiveness.

We combine project structuring, artificial intelligence, digital marketing, branding and automation to help our clients become more visible, efficient and competitive.

The founder

Behind Silamana Business Model AGENCY

Mamoudou Sangaré
Founder & Managing Director — SBM

A consultant in digital transformation, artificial intelligence and digital marketing, Mamoudou Sangaré founded Silamana Business Model AGENCY (SBM) to support companies and project founders around one promise: Create · Automate · Succeed.

He created SBM Academy, SBM's training arm dedicated to digital professions, artificial intelligence, design, digital marketing and business performance, with 100% hands-on courses adapted to the African context. He is also the designer of Guinée Business Connect, a B2B networking and subcontracting platform linking Guinean SMEs with contracting organizations.

  • Professional bachelor's degree in Mining Engineering — Mohamed V University, Conakry (2017)
  • Certifications in AI and data analysis (Google, 2026), digital marketing (2025) and graphic design and video editing (2024)
  • Second Vice-President of CDE.GUI, in charge of Economic Intelligence, Programs, Training and Digital Development
